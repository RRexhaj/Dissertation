S.L. 65.11 — MOTOR VEHICLES REGULATIONS: SPEED LIMITS (REGULATION 127)
Laws of Malta, S.L. 65.11 (Motor Vehicles Regulations)

127. SPEED LIMITS
127(1) No motor vehicle shall exceed the speed limit shown in the table below.
For private cars, taxis, motor vehicles for hire, self-drive cars, motor cycles, motor hearses and invalid carriages:
  (a) In towns and villages (built-up areas): 50 km/h;
  (b) Outside towns or villages (open road): 80 km/h.
For minibuses and goods vehicles:
  (a) In towns and villages: 40 km/h;
  (b) Outside towns or villages: 60 km/h.
Vehicles fitted with solid tyres are limited to 15 km/h in towns and villages and 20 km/h outside.
Note: Malta has no motorways; 80 km/h is the maximum general speed limit on the open road.

127(2) Notwithstanding sub-regulation (1), the Authority for Transport in Malta may prescribe a lower speed limit for any particular road or area (for example 30 km/h or 20 km/h zones near schools, in residential areas, or in pedestrian-shared spaces). Such lower limits are indicated by road signs, and where a speed limit sign is present it overrides the general limits.

127(3)–(11) ENFORCEMENT BY SPEED MONITORING DEVICES
Photographs, film, video-recordings or other records produced by a prescribed speed-monitoring device (speed camera) are admissible in evidence and constitute proof of their contents when accompanied by a certificate signed by a police officer or community officer. The registered owner of the vehicle is responsible for the offence unless the driver at the time was stopped and identified by the police immediately after the offence. A speed-monitoring device is presumed to have been functioning accurately unless the contrary is proved.

RELATED PROVISIONS
- Speed limit signs are those set out in the traffic signs schedule (S.L. 65.10).
- A driver caught speeding by an enforcement camera receives a contravention notice by post with the corresponding penalty points.
- Where excessive speed contributes to an accident, the driver may be prosecuted for dangerous driving under Cap. 65.
