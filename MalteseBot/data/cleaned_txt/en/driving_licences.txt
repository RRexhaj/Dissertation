S.L. 65.18 — MOTOR VEHICLES (DRIVING LICENCES) REGULATIONS (EXTRACTS)
Laws of Malta, S.L. 65.18

REGULATION 2 — CATEGORIES OF DRIVING LICENCE
2(1) Driving licences in Malta are issued in the following categories in accordance with EU Directive 2006/126/EC:
  AM — Mopeds and light quadricycles (max 45 km/h, 50cc or 4kW)
  A1 — Light motorcycles (max 125cc, 11kW)
  A2 — Medium motorcycles (max 35kW)
  A  — All motorcycles (unrestricted)
  B  — Cars and light vans up to 3,500 kg, up to 8 passenger seats
  B+E — Category B with trailer
  C  — Lorries and goods vehicles over 3,500 kg
  D  — Buses and coaches for more than 8 passengers
  BE, CE, DE — Combination vehicles

REGULATION 3 — MINIMUM AGE FOR LICENSING
3(1) The minimum ages for obtaining a driving licence in Malta are:
  (a) Category AM: 16 years
  (b) Category A1: 16 years
  (c) Category A2: 18 years
  (d) Category A: 24 years (or 20 years if holding A2 for at least 2 years)
  (e) Category B: 17 years (learner), 18 years (full licence)
  (f) Category C: 18 years
  (g) Category D: 21 years

REGULATION 4 — LEARNER DRIVERS
4(1) A learner driver must be accompanied at all times by a qualified driving instructor or a person who:
  (a) Holds a full driving licence for category B for at least 3 years;
  (b) Has not had their licence suspended or revoked in the preceding 3 years.
4(2) A learner driver must display an L-plate (red L on white background) on the front and rear of the vehicle.
4(3) A learner driver is subject to the same traffic laws as a full licence holder.

REGULATION 38 — PROBATIONARY (NEW) DRIVERS
38(1) New drivers who pass their driving test are granted a probationary driving licence for a period of three years (S.L. 65.18 art. 38).
38(2) The penalty point system applies to probationary licence holders. A probationary licence is revoked, like a full licence, when the holder accumulates 12 penalty points or more (S.L. 65.18 arts 36C and 40(3)); there is no separate lower points threshold for new drivers.
38(3) New drivers (holders of a probationary licence) are subject to the lower blood alcohol limit of 20 mg per 100 ml of blood (0.02 g/100ml) under Cap. 65 art. 15.
38(4) After a revocation, a new licence may be obtained after two months; repeated revocations may require re-sitting the driving test.

REGULATION 6 — LICENCE VALIDITY AND RENEWAL
6(1) Category B licences issued after 19 January 2013 are valid for 10 years.
6(2) Licences must be renewed before expiry; driving on an expired licence is an offence.
6(3) Licence holders aged 70 or over must renew every 3 years and may be required to pass a medical fitness test.

REGULATION 7 — MEDICAL FITNESS
7(1) Licence holders must inform the licensing authority of any medical condition that may affect their ability to drive safely.
7(2) Failure to disclose a relevant medical condition is an offence.
7(3) The licensing authority may require a medical examination at any time where there are grounds to doubt a driver's fitness.

REGULATION 8 — FOREIGN LICENCES
8(1) Holders of EU/EEA driving licences may drive in Malta without exchanging their licence.
8(2) Non-EU/EEA residents who take up ordinary residence in Malta must exchange their foreign licence within one year.
8(3) Bilateral agreements may allow direct exchange for certain non-EU licence holders without a test.

REGULATION 9 — SUSPENSION AND REVOCATION
9(1) The licensing authority may suspend a licence where the holder accumulates the demerit point threshold under Cap. 65.
9(2) The licensing authority may revoke a licence where:
  (a) The holder has been convicted of a serious traffic offence;
  (b) Medical unfitness has been established;
  (c) The licence was obtained by fraud or misrepresentation.
9(3) A person whose licence is revoked must reapply and pass all relevant tests.
