S.L. 65.32 — MICROMOBILITY REGULATIONS (EXTRACTS)
Laws of Malta, S.L. 65.32

REGULATION 2 — SCOPE
2(1) These regulations apply to:
  (a) Electric scooters with a motor not exceeding 250W and a maximum design speed of 25 km/h;
  (b) Electrically assisted pedal cycles (e-bikes) that assist only when the rider is pedalling;
  (c) Motorised skateboards and hoverboards used on public roads or cycle lanes.
2(2) Vehicles referred to in (a)-(c) are not considered motor vehicles for licensing or registration purposes but are subject to the safety provisions of this subsidiary legislation.

REGULATION 3 — MINIMUM AGE
3(1) A person under the age of sixteen shall not ride or use an electric scooter on a public road, cycle lane, or shared space.
3(2) Electrically assisted pedal cycles may be used by persons who are at least fourteen years of age.
3(3) Parents or guardians are responsible for ensuring compliance with these age requirements.

REGULATION 4 — PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT
4(1) All riders of electric scooters are required to wear an approved helmet conforming to European standard EN 1078 or equivalent.
4(2) Helmets are strongly recommended but not legally mandatory for e-bike riders.

REGULATION 5 — USE RESTRICTIONS
5(1) Electric scooters and electric bicycles shall not:
  (a) Be ridden on pavements or pedestrianised areas unless expressly indicated;
  (b) Be ridden in pedestrian zones marked with sign P2;
  (c) Be ridden on motorways or high-speed roads.
5(2) Where marked cycle lanes exist, their use is mandatory.
5(3) Where no cycle lane exists, use on the road is permitted but road safety remains the rider's responsibility.

REGULATION 6 — SPEED LIMITS
6(1) The maximum permitted speed on cycle lanes is 25 km/h.
6(2) On public roads without a cycle lane, the maximum speed is the same as the prevailing road limit (as signed).
6(3) Near pedestrians or in walking zones, riders shall reduce speed appropriately.

REGULATION 7 — TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS
7(1) Electric scooters shall be fitted with a front and rear light operational at all times of low visibility.
7(2) An audible warning device (bell or buzzer) is required to warn pedestrians.
7(3) Reliable braking (brakes on both front and rear wheels) is mandatory.
7(4) Electric scooters shall not be modified to exceed the 25 km/h speed limit.

REGULATION 8 — PARKING
8(1) Electric scooters shall not be left blocking pavements, pedestrian crossings, or building exits.
8(2) Where dedicated docking stations exist, riders are encouraged to use them.
8(3) Improper parking may result in a fine and removal of the vehicle at the owner's expense.

REGULATION 9 — PENALTIES
9(1) Contravention of these regulations carries fines as set out in the penalty schedule.
9(2) An underage rider may also result in action against the parent or guardian.
9(3) Repeat violations may result in seizure of the vehicle by the police.
