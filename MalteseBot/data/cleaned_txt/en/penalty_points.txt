MALTESE ROAD TRAFFIC PENALTY POINTS SYSTEM
Source: Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) Regulations, S.L. 65.18 arts 36A–40, and the Sixth Schedule

PENALTY POINT SYSTEM — KEY RULES
- Every driver starts with zero penalty points.
- Penalty points for each contravention are imposed by the Court of Magistrates or the Commissioners for Justice and recorded in the Authority's database (S.L. 65.18 art. 36B / 40).
- A full driving licence is revoked if the holder accumulates 12 penalty points or more within any 12-month period (S.L. 65.18 art. 36C).
- The same 12-point threshold applies to holders of a probationary (new-driver) licence (S.L. 65.18 art. 40(3)). New drivers hold a probationary licence for a 3-year period (S.L. 65.18 art. 38).
- After a first revocation, the driver may obtain a new licence after 2 months without re-sitting the driving test (S.L. 65.18 art. 36E(a)).
- A second revocation within three years again requires a 2-month wait and a fresh application; repeated revocations carry progressively longer consequences, up to re-sitting the test.
- A holder with 11 points or fewer may reduce recorded points by 50% by passing an approved safety-awareness test, no more than once every two years (S.L. 65.18 art. 36D).

INDICATIVE OFFENCE / PENALTY POINT SCHEDULE (S.L. 65.18 Sixth Schedule)
The number of points per offence is set out in the Sixth Schedule; indicative values include:

Speeding offences:
- Exceeding the limit by 1-10 km/h: 1 penalty point
- Exceeding the limit by 11-20 km/h: 2 penalty points
- Exceeding the limit by 21-30 km/h: 3 penalty points
- Exceeding the limit by 31-40 km/h: 4 penalty points
- Exceeding the limit by more than 40 km/h: 5 penalty points

Mobile phone and distraction offences:
- Using a hand-held mobile phone while driving: 3 penalty points

Seatbelt offences:
- Failure to wear a seatbelt (driver): 3 penalty points
- Failure to ensure a child passenger is restrained: 3 penalty points

Alcohol and drug offences:
- Driving while exceeding the prescribed alcohol limit: penalty points plus disqualification
- Refusing a breath test when required by police: penalty points equivalent to exceeding the limit
- Driving under the influence of drugs: penalty points plus disqualification

Traffic light and sign offences:
- Failing to stop at a red traffic light: 4 penalty points
- Failing to give way as required by a Give Way sign: 2 penalty points
- Failing to stop at a Stop sign: 3 penalty points

Overtaking offences:
- Dangerous overtaking: 4 penalty points
- Overtaking on a solid white line: 3 penalty points

Other serious offences:
- Failure to stop after a road accident (hit and run): serious offence, penalty points plus possible prosecution
- Driving without a valid driving licence: penalty points plus prosecution
- Driving without valid vehicle insurance: penalty points plus prosecution
- Reckless or dangerous driving: penalty points plus prosecution

FINE STRUCTURE FOR CONTRAVENTIONS (Cap. 65 art. 62 — special procedure)
- Contraventions carry a fixed penalty stated on the notice.
- If not paid within 15 days: the fine doubles.
- If not paid within 30 days: the fine triples.
- Continued non-payment leads to referral to the Commissioner for Justice and potential court proceedings.
